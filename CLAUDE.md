# Web Scraping Automation — Soft Strategy Weekly

## Purpose

This project is the research-and-writing support system for **Soft Strategy Weekly**, a weekly newsletter under the brand **@softlybybelle**. Each issue grounds a single belief or behavioral pattern — the kind of soft, internal stuff (how people think about productivity, money, decisions, self-worth) — in real research, then writes it up in the brand's voice. The job here is to find well-written source material, pull out the ideas and evidence worth citing, and make sure anything specific enough to cite is actually true before it ships.

This project follows the general **WAT framework** (Workflows, Agents, Tools) described in [WAT Claude.md](WAT%20Claude.md), already present in this folder. This file adds the project-specific brief on top of that general framework.

## Mandatory verification step

**Medium is a mixed-quality platform.** Some writers on Medium cite real research properly; others fabricate or misattribute statistics for engagement. Because Medium is this project's primary source, this verification step is not optional — it is the main quality gate for the entire project.

Any specific statistic, study name, or named researcher pulled from a Medium article **must be independently verified before it is used in the newsletter.** Verify it one of two ways:

1. **Web-search the study or author name** to confirm the study actually exists and actually says what the Medium article claims it says, or
2. **Trace back to the original source** the article is citing and confirm it there directly.

**If a claim can't be verified:**
- Drop it, or
- Rewrite the point without the specific number or study name — use softer framing instead of inventing false precision. For example, swap "a 2019 Stanford study found X" for "research suggests X" — but only if it's a claim you can actually stand behind, not just a vaguer version of an unverified one.

Never publish a specific number, study name, or named researcher in the newsletter without having cleared this check first.
